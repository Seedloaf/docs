### Enabling Cracked
1. Go to your server properties tab<br></br>
2. Find and disable <code>Online Mode</code><br></br>
3. Save and restart your server<br></br>

### Joining a Cracked Server
At this time, cracked players cannot join through the official <code>.seedloaf.gg</code> IP. Instead, please join through your secondary IP which can be found on your dashboard tab!
