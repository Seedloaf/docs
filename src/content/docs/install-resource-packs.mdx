---
title: Installing Resource Packs
description: Shows you how to install resource packs for members of your server.
---
To install a resource pack, follow the guide below! Keep in mind that any resource pack you have will override client-side resource packs enabled by your server members.

**Step 1:** Find a Java texture pack [.zip]. Ensure you are not using a Bedrock texture pack [.mcpack]!

**Step 2:** Go to any resource hosting website such as https://mc-packs.net/ and upload the pack.

**Step 3:** Once it's uploaded, copy the given URL and go back to your dashboard.

**Step 4:** Open your server.properties tab and scroll until you find <code>resource-pack=</code>

**Step 5:** Paste your given link and click save.

**Step 6:** Restart your server and enjoy the pack!
