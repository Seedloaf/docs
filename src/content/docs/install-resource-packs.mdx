---
title: Installing Resource Packs
description: Shows you how to install resource packs for members of your server.
---
Below is a guide  on installing resource packs! Before you begin, there are two things to keep in mind. First, all server side packs will override client side packs. Second, this guide is split into two parts (bedrock and java). Make sure you follow the guide relevent to you!

## For Java Members
**Step 1: Find a Pack**
Find a Java texture pack [.zip]. Ensure you are not using a Bedrock texture pack [.mcpack].

**Step 2: Host Your Resource Pack**
Go to any resource hosting website such as https://mc-packs.net/ and upload the pack. Once it's uploaded, copy the given URL and go back to your dashboard.

**Step 3: Add It To Your Server**
Open your server.properties tab and scroll until you find <code>resource-pack=</code> than paste the link you copied and click save!

## For Bedrock Members
**Step 1: Find a Pack**
Find a bedrock texture pack [.mcpack]. Ensure you are not downloading a java texture pack [.zip].

**Step 2: Add It To Your Server**
Locate your bedrock packs folder (plugins -> geyser -> packs) and upload the pack you downloaded there!

### ✅ Enjoy your new texture pack!
